About Us

My Heating and Cooling career started over 15 years ago when I attended the Muskegon Area Career Tech Center for HVAC in my junior and senior year of high school. I then got my Associates degree in HVAC at Ferris State University while working part time in the HVAC trade. I have done both service and installation work for over a decade at a local business in Muskegon and have successfully dealt with difficult problems in residential and commercial equipment.

My wife, our five young children, and I live outside of Whitehall on a small acreage. I enjoy serving the Muskegon area with quality, friendly, and reliable service.
